Questions

What to know before you request a date

How long should I allow?

We normally recommend at least half a day. A longer itinerary gives more time for lunch and nearby stops.

Is it suitable for families?

It can be. Tell us the ages and mobility needs when you enquire so the pace can be adapted.

Do you guarantee every stop?

No. The final route depends on opening hours, weather, availability and the itinerary agreed before departure.
